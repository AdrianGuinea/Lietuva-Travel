Process

From first flight to final check

Each stage runs its own rule. If one fails, the app shows the reason and a fix before you pay.

Step 01

Choose your flight

Pick from nine origin airports. The cheapest fare is found and a 15% student discount applies if you hold an ISIC card.

Step 02

Match your stay

Your arrival time is checked against each hotel's check-in cut-off. Late arrivals only see stays with 24-hour reception.

Step 03

Add experiences

Outdoor activities need clear skies. Live weather for each city decides which tours you can add to the day.

Step 04

Stay within budget

Your total is summed across flights, nights and activities. If you go over, the app suggests a fix before you book.
