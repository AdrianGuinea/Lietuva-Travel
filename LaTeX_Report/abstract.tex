This report documents the correction of three mathematical defects identified by Prof. I.~Grinis in the \emph{Lietuva Travel} categorical travel-planning engine (PHP~8.1+). Although the project's self-reported test suite claimed 15/15 category-theoretic laws verified, three real violations survived: (1) the \texttt{Option} monad's \texttt{pure} constructor conflated the falsy value \texttt{false} with absence of a value, breaking the monad's right-identity law for \texttt{Some(false)}; (2) the \texttt{TripMetrics} monoid rounded its cost to two decimals after every \texttt{append()}, which made the monoid's associativity law fail for sub-cent amounts; and (3) \texttt{Itinerary::advance()} silently discarded a failed path composition in the induced free category $F(G)$, letting an invalid itinerary succeed instead of failing. Each defect is analysed at its root cause, fixed with the smallest change that restores the law, and covered by a new regression assertion added to the existing law-verification suite, which now reports 18/18 laws passing.
