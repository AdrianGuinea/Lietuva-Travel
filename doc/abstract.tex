This report documents the correction of three mathematical defects identified by Prof. I.~Grinis in the \emph{Lietuva Travel} categorical travel-planning engine (PHP~8.1+). Although the project's self-reported test suite claimed all its category-theoretic laws verified, three real violations survived: (1) the \texttt{Option} monad's \texttt{pure} constructor conflated the falsy value \texttt{false} with absence of a value, breaking the monad's right-identity law for \texttt{Some(false)}; (2) the \texttt{TripMetrics} monoid rounded its cost to two decimals after every \texttt{append()}, which made the monoid's associativity law fail for sub-cent amounts; and (3) \texttt{Itinerary::advance()} silently discarded a failed path composition in the induced free category $F(G)$, letting an invalid itinerary succeed instead of failing. Each defect is analysed at its root cause and fixed with the smallest change that restores the law. The project's additional invariant suite, \texttt{LietuvaTravel\_regression\_checks.php}, independently exercises the \texttt{Option} monad, the \texttt{TripMetrics} monoid, and the free-category path/itinerary machinery under edge-case inputs (nulls, sparse arrays, mismatched graph edges), reporting 10/10 checks passing.
